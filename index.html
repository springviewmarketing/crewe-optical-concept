Is my eye test really free?
Yes. Eye tests at Crewe Optical are free for all NHS and private patients, and they include a free OCT scan.
What is an OCT scan?
OCT (optical coherence tomography) takes a detailed, layered image of the back of your eye. It helps your optometrist look for early signs of conditions such as glaucoma and macular degeneration. It’s quick, and nothing touches your eye.
Am I eligible for an NHS eye test?
NHS-funded eye tests are available to eligible patients, including children under 16, adults over 60, people with diabetes or glaucoma, and those receiving Universal Credit, Pension Credit or other qualifying benefits. Call us and we’ll help you check.
Can I get help paying for glasses?
Some patients may be eligible for free or discounted glasses under the NHS optical voucher scheme. Our team will guide you through your options.
What should I do if I have a sudden eye problem?
Call us. We’re part of the CUES and PEARS schemes, so we can help with sudden vision changes, eye pain, flashes, floaters and infections, often faster than a GP or hospital appointment. For a serious eye injury or sudden loss of sight, go to A&E or call 999.
Do you have glasses for children?
Yes. Our range includes children’s styles that are as practical as they are fun, alongside designer eyewear and affordable everyday glasses for adults.
How do I book?
Complete our short online booking form, or call us on 01270 695 835.
Are you open on Sundays?
Yes. We’re open Monday to Saturday from 9am to 5pm, and on Sundays from 10am to 4pm.
